Nothing you
do not need.

No pitch fader, no reverse, no USB. A machined platter, a belt, a bearing and an arm with a counterweight you set once.

The plinth is solid walnut, oak or ash. It is heavy for a reason: mass is the cheapest way to keep a record quiet.

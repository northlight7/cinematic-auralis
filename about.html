Three rules,
then stop.

Every decision on the AR-01 had to pass three tests. If it did not, it did not go on the deck.

Does it help the needle?

Speed stability, arm geometry and plinth mass all serve one thing: the stylus sitting still in a groove.

Can it be repaired?

The motor, bearing and arm are all replaceable with hand tools. No potting, no glue.

Will it look right in ten years?

Hardwood, metal and one orange label. Nothing that dates.

Made to be
kept.

The plinth is oiled rather than lacquered, so a scratch can be sanded out instead of hidden. The bearing is a standard size. The arm wand is a tube you could replace yourself.

A turntable should outlast the records you play on it, and then keep going for whoever gets it next.
